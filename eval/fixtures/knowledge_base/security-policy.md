# Security Policy

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all production systems. The SEC-44 recovery process applies when a hardware token is lost, damaged, or unavailable during travel.

Customer PII must be redacted before export to external vendors. The export owner must record the approval ticket, destination, retention period, and deletion date before any transfer begins.

A SEV-1 incident must be declared within fifteen minutes when customer data, production availability, or payment flows are at risk. The incident commander notifies legal, support, and the executive duty officer.

Security reviews are required before enabling public network access, new OAuth scopes, or third-party analytics scripts.
