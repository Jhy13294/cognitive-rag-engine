# Finance Policy

The EXP-204 mileage reimbursement policy covers approved customer travel. Employees must submit route evidence, business purpose, and manager approval; fuel receipts are optional when mileage is reimbursed by distance.

Invoices above 5000 USD require approval from the budget owner and finance controller. Invoices below that threshold may be approved by the direct department owner if the vendor is already registered.

Travel receipts must be submitted within seven calendar days after the trip ends. Missing hotel receipts require a written exception from finance operations.

Quarterly budget reviews compare forecast, committed spend, and actual spend. The finance policy does not list payroll tax tables.
