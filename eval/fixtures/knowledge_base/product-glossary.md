# Product Glossary

Nimbus Gateway is the edge service that routes IoT device telemetry into regional ingestion clusters. It validates device certificates, normalizes payload headers, and forwards events to the streaming bus.

Vega Connector is the partner integration layer for CRM exports. It maps account fields, applies consent flags, and writes delivery audit records for each sync job.

Apollo Dashboard is the customer success console used to monitor renewal risk, adoption trends, and open escalation signals. Account teams review Apollo before quarterly business reviews.

The product glossary defines product names and internal service responsibilities. It does not describe Kubernetes autoscaling rules.
